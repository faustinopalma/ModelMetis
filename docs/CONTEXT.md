## Data Visibility Contract

Masking means excluding information from the model's accessible data, not asking it to ignore information that it can read. A taxonomy and task instructions may be disclosed. Sample answers may enter only through the explicitly authorized support-set export; the general ingestion path must not disclose query labels.

| Consumer | Allowed inputs | Forbidden inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Import/audit process | Publisher files, manifests, license, original labels and acquisition metadata | Unrestricted publication of source mappings or gold labels |
| Teacher inference | Canonical query audio, taxonomy/context, explicitly authorized disjoint audio/label support examples with provenance | Query reference labels, other publisher labels, original filenames/paths, fault-coded IDs, source captions, unrestricted retrieval |
| Student training | Training-partition audio, teacher-produced silver labels, separately obtained authorized human corrections, provenance and weights | Publisher reference labels, held-out audio/labels, source mappings, evaluator output containing example-level truth |
| Supervised diagnostic control (EXP-007 only) | Dedicated training audio and matching publisher training labels, or the authorized disjoint support package | Query labels/audio in fitting, fake silver provenance, operational promotion or routing authority |
| Strategy coordinator | Training evidence, bounded development metrics, costs, latency, coverage, experiment lineage | Final gold labels, per-example gold errors, source lookup tools, evaluator credentials |
| Blind reviewer | Sanitized sample, task taxonomy, authorized operational context | Publisher reference labels and label-coded metadata; model predictions also hidden when measuring independent review |
| Sealed evaluator | Frozen candidate predictions and corresponding hidden references in an isolated role | Authority to relabel training data, alter the candidate, or promote a release |

Operational sample IDs must be random, opaque, and independent of source labels. Keep the reversible source-to-sample mapping in a separate restricted store, not a column returned by an operational API. Renaming a file alone is insufficient: strip answer-bearing container metadata and sidecars, use sanitized object names and display names, and exclude source labels from prompts, logs, traces, exports, caches, and embeddings. Content hashes support audit/deduplication but need not be exposed to the model.

Split by original acquisition and physical unit before segmentation or augmentation. CSV/MAT copies, windows, channels, and noise variants of the same source cannot cross partitions. Preserve grouping in an internal split manifest without exposing label-coded group IDs as features. Audit whether motor identity determines class; if independent fault generalization cannot be tested, report that limitation rather than claiming it.

Use distinct training, calibration/development, and final gold partitions. Teacher labeling for learning operates on training data only. Held-out audio may be sent to a frozen model for evaluation, but the request and result must not enter the collection or training path. The coordinator may compare candidates on a bounded development protocol; final gold is not an iterative reward or prompt-tuning source. Freeze the strategy and promotion criteria before the final gold run and record every access.

Publisher labels are never automatically converted into `silver` or `verified` operational labels. The authorized EXP-006 support package uses the distinct provenance `simulated_human_from_publisher`; it does not claim an actual reviewer. Its ordered audio/label digest is bound into the prompt hash, and overlap with query samples/groups/audio is rejected before requests. A teacher label remains fallible silver; agreement is not independent verification. Genuine human correction records its author, method and revision. Public benchmarks may have appeared in foundation-model pretraining; masking does not prove the teacher has never seen the dataset.

## Required Learning Cycle

1. Audit the source and rights in a restricted import process. Establish an independently evaluable taxonomy and grouped partitions; do not infer class combinations solely from the filename legend.
2. Create sanitized, unlabeled operational samples. Prove the absence of source-label leakage before connecting a teacher or permitting training access.
3. Select and evaluate a real audio-capable teacher. Benchmark mechanical fault reasoning, schema compliance, abstention, latency, and cost; audio support alone is insufficient. No transcription-only substitute for non-speech diagnosis without evidence.
4. Start with teacher-only serving. Collect predictions, failures, costs, and immutable provenance. Escalate ambiguous or critical cases for blind review; do not force a label when the teacher cannot resolve a sample.
5. Let the coordinator propose bounded experiments such as prompt/teacher variants, audio representations, training-set selection, label-quality filters, and specialist families. Record hypothesis, data version, cost budget, stopping rule, and result for each attempt. Initial candidate families are hypotheses, not approved choices.
6. Train students from the collected operational labels, not publisher annotations. Preserve label provenance and prevent held-out examples from entering snapshots.
7. Compare frozen candidates against the current policy using independent development evaluation, calibration, critical-class recall, abstention/coverage, latency, and cost per valid decision. Count teacher retries/fallback, experiments, training, and idle serving costs, not only per-call model prices.
8. Run final evaluation, then shadow and approved canary stages. Promote only on valid evidence; retain teacher fallback and a tested rollback. More data permits another experiment, not automatic promotion.

The lifecycle remains `teacher_only -> collecting -> training -> offline_evaluation -> shadow -> canary -> specialized_default`. Training and failed experiments do not replace the last approved serving policy. Model and infrastructure operations use different identities and permissions.

Data-boundary tests must include a positive control: plant known forbidden values and show that an intentionally contaminated path is rejected. Test semantic/parsed JSON fields and escaped representations, not only literal substrings. Also test RIFF metadata, source paths, review notes, sidecars, derived windows, and equal-source variants. An empty export is not a passing leak test; assert nonzero sample and invocation counts.

Future private-data verification must include both sides of the boundary: a successful read/write from the authorized VNet workload and a denied public data-plane operation.
