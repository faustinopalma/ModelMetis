Figures are deterministic DSP renderings of these derivatives produced by ModelMetis. The interpretations and conclusions are ModelMetis's own and carry no endorsement by the source authors. Labels are the publishers' annotations.

This release has twelve references (four Jin front-microphone windows, eight Ottawa profile-1 unloaded acquisitions) and twenty-eight query excerpts (twelve Jin right-microphone windows, sixteen Ottawa profile-2 acquisitions). [Per-record provenance](provenance.json) lists their hashes. The release includes the selected microphone excerpts only.
