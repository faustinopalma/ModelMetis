Figures are deterministic DSP renderings of these derivatives produced by ModelMetis. Source authors do not endorse the model, interpretations or diagnostic conclusions. Labels are publisher annotations, not new expert diagnoses.

[Per-record provenance](provenance.json) lists collection, class, audio hash, rate, duration and figure hashes. This release has twelve references and four query excerpts. It omits complete datasets, smartphone/noise variants, accelerometer/temperature channels and unselected recordings.
